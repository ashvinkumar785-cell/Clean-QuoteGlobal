How this cleaning business pricing calculator works

This calculator adds labor, payroll costs, supplies, travel and overhead to get your true job cost, then applies your target profit margin (price = cost ÷ (1 − margin)) so that profit is a real percentage of the price, not a guess.

Currency conversion uses fixed approximate rates, so treat results as a guide and adjust the amounts to your local market.
