Frequently Asked Questions

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. All tools are free, require no account and have no usage limits.

How accurate are the quotes?

They are estimates based on typical industry formulas. Real prices depend on your location, provider, and the home itself.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AUD and CAD. Rates are fixed approximations and can be edited in app.js.

Do you store my data?

No. Calculations happen entirely in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

How much does a deep clean cost compared to a regular clean?

Usually 1.5 to 2 times more, because it takes longer and covers areas that routine cleaning skips.

How should a cleaning business set its prices?

Add labor, payroll costs, supplies, travel and overhead, then apply your target profit margin. Our Cleaning Business Pricing Calculator does this for you.
